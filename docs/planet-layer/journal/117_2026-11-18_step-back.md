**What the land median misses.** Adding sediment at the start lowered median land height from 503 to 456 m (Earth 380 m). Inland lowland increased only from 5.5% to 5.9%, while the 90th percentile rose from 1,499 to 1,684 m. Much of the low continental surface is submerged. On the common datum, 31% of continent with crust at least 30 km thick lies below zero, against Earth's 11%. The six-seed decomposition assigns +95 m to the spread of continental heights, +25 m to sea level and −49 m to the continents' overall level. Across the ocean, the depth is credible even with excess young floor. Production averages 5.25 km²/yr over the last 100 Myr against the 2.5–4.5 band. Ocean–ocean convergence length is close to Earth's, but the model classifies all of it as trench.
