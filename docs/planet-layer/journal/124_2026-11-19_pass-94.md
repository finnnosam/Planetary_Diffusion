#### 3. The rest: the column's water load (`rest_split.txt`)

On the planet `rest` = z − (Airy + dens + sedc + C + dyn): the flexure's departure from local Airy and the water
loads. `surface.elevation` makes three water-load decisions per continental node: the crust's own Airy depth is
water-loaded (× ρw / (ρm − ρw)) wherever it lies below sea level (line 180); the sediment is dry where its bulk top
stands above the crust's *water-loaded* level; C by the final surface. On sediment-filled thinned crust the crust is
water-loaded under a sediment load booked dry: no water is in that column. In 1-D Airy a column has one water load,
decided by its air-loaded local level (z_air < sea → z = sea + (z_air − sea) ρm / (ρm − ρw)); the model's parts agree
with it only when every part is wet.

| bin km | planet rest = crust water term (on dry columns) + C water + flexure | bin dry with crust below sea | planet z − z_loc | Earth z − z_loc |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 25–30 | −590 = −812 (−672) + 84 + 139 | 0.76 | −470 | +360 |
| 30–35 | −394 = −366 (−306) + 10 − 38 | 0.72 | −327 | +229 |
| 35–40 | −134 = −32 (−31) − 44 − 58 | 0.23 | −99 | +82 |
| 40–45 | −64 = −2 (−2) − 24 − 37 | 0.01 | −53 | −290 |
| 30–45 | −189 = −117 (−99) − 25 − 47 | 0.32 | −150 | **−4** |
| all ≥ 20 | −240 = −250 (−200) − 2 + 11 | 0.36 | −178 | +105 |

z_loc: the local column with one water decision, alike for both (Earth's Holdt dyn taken out). **Over 30–45 km Earth
stands on that law within 4 m; the planet stands 150 m below it**, 99 m of which is the crust's water term on dry
columns. The model's own gate already reads this law: `elevation_check` water-loads the whole column once (its line
64), which is why V2′'s thin-crust offset reads −173 … −230 m on today's model (and −98 … −180 on the control before
back-arc): the gate has been measuring the defect inside its ±300 m band. The planet's level gap over 30–45 km (area
means 85 against 411 m) is then about half the law (−150) and half the column (z_loc 235 against 415: the cover on
normal crust, C, dyn).

The term was written for the thinned crust before continental sediment existed (pass 18; sediment as its own layer
pass 20, compaction pass 37); its docstring says "its own Airy depth only, not its neighbours'": right without
sediment, wrong under a dry fill.

#### 4. Built: `water_load_rule`

`params.py`: `water_load_rule` 'parts' (as built, default) / 'column'. `surface.elevation` under 'column': wet_col =
continental & (own_airy + sediment bulk height − its dry mass / ρm < 0) (the column's air-loaded local level against
the previous sea level, as the other terms use); the sediment is dry where the column is dry; the crust's water term
only on wet columns; C unchanged (by the final surface). In the Airy limit wet columns give z_air ρm / (ρm − ρw) and dry
ones z_air: one law, continuous at sea level (the as-built law jumps). No value. 'parts' keeps the same expressions.
