**4. Why C is back, and why now.** Pass 34 (journal 50) set C aside: inside Bird's orogens Earth's thick crust stands
on the planet's Airy line, the planet's thick crust was then almost all young orogen, so C "would lower nothing the
planet has much of". With the collapse flow off the plateaus are gone; what stands too high is the inherited
interior (0.63 of the continent), the crust Earth holds below the Airy line. The circumstance changed; the earlier
reading holds for what it measured.

C from measured values, sized before any build:
- Lithosphere thickness by tectonothermal age, Artemieva (2006, TC1, Tectonophysics 416, 245–277): z = 0.04 t + 93.6
  km (t in Ma): ~94–102 km for crust reworked in the last 200 Myr, 134 km at 1 Ga, 154 at 1.5 Ga, ~200 and > 250 km
  for Archean cratons.
- Density contrast of the mantle lithosphere against the asthenosphere, Lamb et al. (2020, G-cubed, "Global whole
  lithosphere isostasy"): 20–40 kg/m³ (net of depletion; the mantle lithosphere's negative buoyancy counters the
  crust's).
- Margins (rifted in the last ~200 Myr, thinner still while young) against interiors (1–2.8 Ga): ΔL ~ 40–110 km, Δz =
  Δρ/ρa · ΔL = 240–1,330 m, ~680 m at the central values (30 kg/m³, 75 km). Earth's −773 m lies inside. A measured
  mechanism reproduces the measured fall's size without a fitted value. Part of Earth's coastal +407 m is young
  orogens and arcs on coasts (Andes, Japan), which a thermal age covers as well.

Export: snapshots carry no erosion field, so where the extra export comes from is not measured here. The interiors and
the steep belts both drain to the sea; lowering the interior ~300–400 m against the coast cuts the relief the
interior's rivers erode. The C sweep tests whether that is enough; if export still fails with C, the next question is
the erosion rate of the narrow belts against Earth's orogens.

**6. C's design and test set (to build in pass 56; the bands are fixed when the `.cmd` is written, before the runs).**
- State: a thermal age per continental node (Myr since its last thermal event), carried with the plates like
  `crust_age_myr`.
- Start: Earth's tectonothermal-age classes from CRUST1.0's types (on disk, `CNtype1_key.txt`; shares in
  `earth-lithosphere-residual-n217.json`: Archean 0.13, Proterozoic 0.32, platform 0.06, Phanerozoic 0.06, orogen
  0.14, arc 0.06, extended 0.05, margin 0.18), joined to the start crust by thickness rank within Earth's joint
  distribution. Ages per class from TC1's classes (to source in pass 56; no fitted value).
- Resets: a collision contact (real convergence on a collision boundary, not the belt clock, which resets on thickness
  alone), rifting (thinning), new continental crust (arcs turned continental). Age 0 → L = 93.6 km.
- Elevation: Δz = −(Δρ/ρa)(L(t) − L_ref), Δρ 30 kg/m³ (Lamb's central value; 20 and 40 as a sensitivity probe, not
  arms to pick from), local compensation (the load is broader than the flexural wavelength); water-loaded where under
  the sea. L_ref = Earth's area mean of L over its continent from the same class map, so Earth-like old crust stays at
  the datum calibration (cont_ref 38 km at +300 m came from Earth's 35–40 km bin).
- Tests: C1 coast-to-interior fall of z − Airy (Earth −773 m; proposed band −390 … −1,160, half to 1.5×); C2 P5 rise
  ≤ 772 m; P1–P4 kept (no worse than their bands); guards as the Collapse Flow Sweep (gates, volume ≥ −14%, export 4–8,
  margins, plates ≤ 40, ledgers) plus thin margins not drowned further (continental crust drowned reported). The C1
  review is `coast_budget.py` promoted into `planet_reviews` with a test.
- Arms (proposal): combo-02 + C, combo-03 + C; the two bases are this sweep's combo-02 / combo-03 (identity per seed
