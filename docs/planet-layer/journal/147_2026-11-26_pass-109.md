#### 2. The cause (cloud, the warm state of seed 1028)

`factor_probe.py` logs every `_conserved` call of the return (the gain factor world ÷ plate volume, the same for the
losses). Over six steps under 'overlap': medians 1.02–1.04, but gain factors up to 7×10⁵, 1.3×10¹⁴ and ∞, loss factors
up to 2.9×10¹⁴ and ∞; plate crust reached −1,694 m and 127 km within six steps. Mechanism: erosion cuts one node and
fills its neighbour; the remap's weighted mean averages the pair, so the plate side keeps a near-zero remainder of one
sign while the world side books the full gain and the full loss; rescaling the gains and the losses each to the world's
volume multiplies that remainder by the ratio. Journal 145 §5 had added per-crust-type conservation, which did not touch
this. (Pass 107's 'conservative' used the same rescaling with a forward map that does not average, which is why it
behaved better on this score.) Under 'nearest' there is no rescaling and no such path.

A first fix (net volume per crust type by an additive correction) removed the blow-up but left a slower ratchet:
`neg_probe.py` found 1–6 continental plate nodes with almost no crust (the world view showed 0.3–6 km over them) running
to −1,793 m in eight steps. The area-mean view hides a near-bare node, so erosion keeps taking from it.

#### 3. The fix (built into the same arm; the rule of the sweep unchanged)

- `_tent_return`: each plate's **net** volume per crust type equals the world's over the nodes of that type it owns, by
  an additive correction spread by area (no multiplicative factor). World nodes are typed by the crust they read at the
  composition (`self._tent_type`), not by the world's type after the step's events. The correction moved 0.43–0.58 Mkm³
  of crust per step on the warm state (about 5% of the step's gross change; overlap weights do not balance exactly).
- `_update_rasters` ('tent' / 'overlap'): plate crust is clamped at zero after the return, as the sediment already is;
  the clamped volume is booked in `erosion_totals['return_thick_clamped_m3']` (warm state: ~1,200 km³ per step, about
  0.04% of crystalline erosion; over the seed-1031 run 0.0014 km³ in total).
- Tests (`tests/test_remap_tent.py`, 15 of 15; with `test_return_transfer` 26 of 26): the sediment test now asks for the
  net volume per plate and crust type (to float32 storage precision, 2×10⁻⁴); new: a node-scale cut next to a fill
  (±300 m, net ~0) comes back finite and within 400 m (pass 108's code gives 492 m at n = 48 and fails); plate crust
  never below zero under a 500 m loss, the clamp booked.
- Warm-state check (`fix_probe.py`, eight steps): plate crust min 0, max 97–128 km against the control's 97–130 km.
