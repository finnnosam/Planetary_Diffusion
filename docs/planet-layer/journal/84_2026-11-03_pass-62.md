**1. Why 'earth_full' is not built** (`earth_by_d`, `planet_by_d`, `edge_budget`, `edge_history`).
- Earth's 20–30 km crystalline crust (42.7 Mkm²) lies 22.2 within 200 km of the label-free edge, 11.8 at 200–500 km
  and 8.6 beyond. Earth's label-free continent has 38.7 Mkm² within 200 km of an edge (305.8k km of edge at 46 km);
  the start supercontinent has 8.2–9.4 (58.5–67.6k km of edge; seeds 1001, 1011 and the control's six).
- By distance rank (the design's "the start's area, volume and composition are Earth's", with crystalline 20–30 km ≥
  0.8 × 42.7 Mkm²), Earth's outer ~40 Mkm² of margin crust would reach ~1,000 km into the supercontinent (seed 1011:
  42 Mkm² lie within 1,000 km of its edge): a thin rim no supercontinent has. By distance in km, the start can carry
  at most Earth's 72.7 km² per km of edge within 200 km on its 58–68k km of edge, ~4–5 Mkm² against 2.1 today.
- The run makes its own margins (freeze run): the start's edge turns active within 10 Myr (passive 58.5k → 50.3k km,
  active 0 → 77.0k km at −240 Myr); passive edge then grows to 127.6k km at 0 Myr, and crystalline crust < 30 km
  within 500 km of a passive edge from 1.3 to 19.0 Mkm² (Earth 33.7; per km of passive edge 22.5 → 149, Earth 195 at
  46 km). What the start puts on its edge is reworked or consumed; the end's thin crust is the run's.
- On Earth too: most of today's shelf, slope and extended crust was made by Pangea's breakup, and before it that
  crust was full-thickness crust on less area. A start carrying today's margins is a start that has already rifted.
- Still realism in the design, kept as a small item: the start's continents and ocean floor carry no sediment at all
  (Earth's continents 320 Mkm³, CRUST1.0; its floor ~0.6 km, GlobSed). Whether the bare start floor sets part of the
  start's low sea (−353 m on the datum on the control at −250 Myr, −285 on combo-02) is not measured.
