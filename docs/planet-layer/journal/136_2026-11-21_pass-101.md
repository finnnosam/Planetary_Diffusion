#### 3. What Earth's slabs explain (`earth_degree_corr.py`, `reader_check.py`, `e3_thermo.py`)

E3 (journal 70) runs Müller et al. (2019)'s subduction history through D's unchanged record and kernels. Pass 50
saved its present-day load layers; `e3_thermo.py` applies today's `slab_alpha` 'thermo' (rms 645 m, as journal 72;
the 'depth' version reproduces pass 49's `e3_H.npz` to 3·10⁻⁵ m). Earth's measured field: Valentine & Davies (2020)'s
posterior-mean coefficients, the file `measured_power_air_m2` already reads for its power.

- **The reader.** The file gives real harmonic coefficients without saying the convention. Of the eight readings
  (sine term on m < 0 or m > 0, Condon–Shortley phase or not, sign of the sine term), one agrees with Holdt et al.
  (2022)'s independent degree 1–30 grid on CRUST1.0's oceanic nodes, where both rest on measurements: r **0.64**,
  against ≤ 0.22 for every other and ≤ 0.11 for every north–south mirror (cosine on m < 0, sine on m > 0, with the
  Condon–Shortley phase). That reading is used; it is a check of the reader, not a fit.
- **The degree correlation r_l** of VD2020 with E3: l 1 / 2 / 3 **+1.00 / +0.88 / +0.32**; l 4–30 scattered about zero
  inside the band of unrelated fields (±1.96/√(2l+1)); bands l 1–3 +0.77, **4–10 +0.07, 11–30 +0.00**. The tomographic
  slab proxy E1 (SMEAN2 fast part below 300 km, the same kernels) cross-checks: +0.76 / +0.21 / +0.09.
- So on Earth the deep slabs' flow accounts for degrees 1–2 of the residual topography and little else. The planet's
  `flow_power` 'measured' gives the slab pattern Earth's whole power at every degree: at l 4–10 that is the
  near-trench lows on the continents (§2) and the interiors' highs (§1), a pattern Earth's own slab flow does not show
  in Earth's measured field.
- **Caveat** (`earth_pb.py`, `planet_pb.py`): corr(field, distance to subduction), like for like on the whole air
  field by band: Earth (VD2020 mean; Bird's SUB) l 1–3 / 4–10 / 11–30 +0.46 / **+0.23** / −0.00, whole +0.46 (Holdt
  1–10 / 11–30 / 1–30 +0.15 / +0.03 / +0.17); the planet +0.31 / **+0.21** / +0.05, whole +0.33. Earth's l 4–10 does have
  some lows near trenches (in the oceans; Holdt's continental points near trenches are not low, §2) that E3's slab flow
  does not reproduce: something other than the deep slabs (the slab above the 300 km cut, the incoming plate) puts them
  there. Under an arm that unplaces l 4–10 the planet loses that correlation. The arm below rests on the degree
  correlation, the direct measurement of how much of Earth's field D's mechanism explains; the caveat is read on both
  arms.

#### 5. Built: `flow_placed` (PARAMS, default 'all')

- 'all': today's model. 'earth_corr' (with `dyn_rule` 'slabs' and `flow_power` 'measured' only; any other pairing
  raises): `MantleFlow` rescales each degree of the flow to `placed_share(l)` × Earth's measured power, `placed_share`
  = max(r_l, 0)² from `mantle.EARTH_SLAB_CORR` (the 30 values of §3, E3 with 'thermo'): 0.99 / 0.78 / 0.10 at l 1–3,
  0.193 of Earth's whole power, 0.040 of l 4–10, 0.011 of l 11–30. `Mantle` draws the rest, power (1 − share) per
  degree, as plate-blind knot fields like `dyn_rule` 'measured''s, from the same stream after the start's field (so
  the start stays the control's and the rest is independent of it), blended between knots at constant variance; after
  the handover the field is the placed flow plus the rest. The expected power per degree stays Earth's. No number
  fitted: the share is Earth's measured correlation of its own field with the same mechanism; negative r_l clip to
  zero (no anti-placement).
- Snapshots: `flow_topography_alm_m` is the placed part only under 'earth_corr'; `dynamic_topography_air_m` is the
  whole field (contract `plate-snapshots.md` updated). `planet_reviews.dynamic_topography` reads the spectrum and P-b
  on the whole air field when `flow_placed` is not 'all' and prints the placed part's P-b beside it.
- `crust_review.PREDATING` gains `flow_placed` 'all'. `tests/test_flow_placed.py` (4: the share table; the flow's power
  is the share of the measured power, its pattern the flow's; the start is the control's and the rest plate-blind,
  independent of the start, with the power the slabs leave; default and bad values), in `run_sweep.PLANET_TESTS`.
  Cloud: `test_flow_placed`, `test_flow_power`, `test_dyn_measured` 13 passed.
- Default path identical step by step to HEAD 173ad27: 71 of 71 states (n = 48, 70 steps, threads 1, every array of
  `state()`); an 'earth_corr' run of the same length completes.
- Two probes changed after their first run, the same numbers to a few metres: `degree_split.py` and `planet_pb.py`
  read the bands on the whole air field by quadrature (under 'earth_corr' the flow's coefficients hold only the placed
  share).
