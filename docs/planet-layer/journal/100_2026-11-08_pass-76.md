**4. Candidates considered.**
- (a) **Deep-crust densification** (built, below): the deepest 0.324 of the crystalline crust (CRUST1.0 types; area
  mean over continental types; 2,947 kg/m³; the share is 0.323–0.326 in every thickness bin 30–55 km) gains density
  linearly in pressure from 1.0 GPa (garnet in: gabbro → garnet granulite, Green & Ringwood 1967, experimental) to the
  mantle's 3,300 kg/m³ at 2.0 GPa (Chapman et al. 2019, the source of `eclogite_rule`'s 65 km). Equilibrium assumed:
  dry granulite can stay metastable (Jackson et al. 2004), so this is the upper bound. It removes an inconsistency:
  the model founders a root at 65 km because it is as dense as the mantle there, yet carries it at 2,830 kg/m³ until
  then. Static estimate (pelagic arm, 0 Myr, local compensation): 35–40 km −7 m, 40–45 km −56 m, 45–55 km −244 m,
  55–80 km −789 m; land p50 1,170 → 1,142 m, p90 2,749 → 2,509 m. It moves the 45–55 km bin toward Earth (on the
  datum 2,066 → 1,826 m; Earth 885) and the 55–80 km bin away (3,535 → 2,777; Earth 4,356 m, Tibet and the Altiplano,
  whose lower crust is partly metastable and hot). Built anyway (working rule: a candidate re-aimed before it is built
  is built; the sweep decides).
- (b) **Mantle lithosphere thickening with shortened crust** (pure shear, the mirror of C's stretching rule): not
  built. C resets the lithosphere's age to 0 at a collision (journal 78), which lifts young belts; Earth's orogens do
  stand above its cratons at equal crust (inside / outside orogens, 40–45 km: 890 / 329 m), and the planet's young /
  old contrast at 40–45 km (1,976 / 1,320 m above sea) is Earth's within ~100 m. Thickening the lithosphere there
  would lower young belts below old cratons, against Earth.
- (c) **The interior's lithosphere** (the larger share, §3): Earth's old interiors sit lower than Artemieva's age law
  and Lamb's 30 kg/m³ give. Values must come from Earth's measured lithosphere, not from fitting C1: Lamb et al.
  (2020, G-cubed) publish their whole-lithosphere-isostasy datasets (Zenodo 4031646): a table of continental interiors
  with measured mean elevation, lithosphere thickness (surface waves) and crustal thickness with 1σ (ds02, 1.7 kB),
  and 1° grids of the elevation anomaly and the density / thickness perturbations that null it. Neither shell can
  fetch it: **asked of the user** (data only the user can get). With it the next arm can be built from Earth's
  measured L against age and crust, independent of CRUST1.0's thickness errors.
