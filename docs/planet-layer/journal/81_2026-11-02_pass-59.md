**4. Export, on combo-02 (9.9 km³/yr against 4–8).**

(a) One-step replay (`precheck-p59-export/export_source.py`; rule in its header, fixed before it ran). PlateSim's own
erosion loop (4 substeps, PARAMS' law) applied once to each run's present surface, and to Earth's ETOPO surface on the
same grid with Bird's orogens as young orogens; shift-share of the excess into terrain and rate. Sanity check first:
the planet's one-step net loss of land volume within ±35% of its run's export. **It fails**: combo-02 seed 1001 gives
1.0 km³/yr against 10.1 (Earth 0.64 with the ice regions out). By the rule the probe is not read. What the failure shows:
in a run the law acts each step on a surface that this step's tectonics have just raised (`sim.step`: rift,
flow, subduction thickening, collisions in the composition, then `_erode`), and the snapshot is taken after the
erosion. Replayed on the eroded surface, the law finds a tenth of the relief it removes in a run. The export is fed by
the tectonic supply each step, as the 18 Oct sweep found ("export barely responds to K: supply-limited"). The narrow
belts' erosion rate is therefore not readable from snapshots: it has to be recorded where it happens.

thickening and the surface the law saw; output only, the model unchanged). Cloud seed 1009 (never used before; a
