### Sixty-first pass (3 Nov): S, the margin wedge, built (default off, default path identical) and pre-checked; not swept. Where the wedge is allowed it builds Earth's continental rise to the measured profile, but its room is a reservoir of ~10–25 Mkm³ against a sediment route of 5–8 km³/yr: on the same state it holds 0.55 of 8.25 km³/yr (7%) and moves nothing the tests read (the far-basin share 0.483 → 0.472, trench sediment unchanged). A six-seed sweep cannot resolve that against the drift, so it was not written to run. A second probe finds the area deficit lies in crystalline 20–30 km crust (Earth 42.7 Mkm², planet 19.6; the start 10.3), not in sediment storage: the planet already carries more sediment on its continents than Earth (446 against 320 Mkm³). Next: the start's crust (Earth's full distribution, margins included)

At the snapshots the wedge's room (ceiling above the floor on eligible nodes) is 12–24 Mkm³ (`d1.py`), on 9–18 of the
~50 Mkm² of ocean beyond the shelf within 300 km of the continental edge: the other 30–40 Mkm² lie within 300 km of
a plate boundary. Earth has ~30 Mkm² of such passive floor; the planet's continents sit closer to its boundaries.
**Reading:** S is a small reservoir on a large flux. Its whole room (12–24 Mkm³) is 1.5–3 Myr of the planet's
deep-sea supply (~8 km³/yr), and Earth's rise stands only ~8 Mkm³ above the planet's floor (the profile difference
× the planet's passive areas, journal 82's table). The sediment route (5–8 km³/yr leaving the continents over 250 Myr, ~1,400 Mkm³) cannot be
housed by the rise's geometry; it is set by how much leaves the continental crust, not by where the deep sea puts
it.

**5. Decision (Claude's, by the rules): S is kept built and off; no sweep.** The rule fixed in the draft
`Margin Wedge Sweep.cmd` asked S2 and S3 to fall against the control on six-seed means. The paired effect (−0.011 on
S2, nil on S3) is a tenth of the drift one seed shows between two arms that differ by nothing measurable (the first
build against the control: S2 0.21 → 0.09), so the sweep could not decide, and its outcome is known: S does not move the volume. The draft
`.cmd` is not copied to the device. S returns with a later bundle as the rise's geometry (a realism detail: it puts
Earth's rise where Earth has one), together with whatever lowers the flux. The S tests (S1–S4) stay as the
sediment route's tests (`planet_reviews.margin_wedge`).

- The missing area is crystalline 20–30 km crust: 19.6 against 42.7 Mkm² (−23.1 of the 27.2 Mkm² deficit). The start
