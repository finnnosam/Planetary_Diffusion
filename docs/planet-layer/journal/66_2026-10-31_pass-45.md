### Step 8, follow-up 2, forty-fifth pass (31 Oct): D designed as an instantaneous mantle flow from the slab record (propagator kernels, Earth viscosity), not a kinematic kernel. Earth pre-check: slabs alone give Earth-shaped long-wavelength topography and lift plate coherence from the null to about 0, a third of Earth's excess; Earth's slow continental rifts do not respond. Tests fixed; Earth inputs in `data/mantle/` (no download); no code changed; build next

The work is STATE's "next" after pass 44: the D design pass. Choose between journal 55's kinematic kernel and an
instantaneous flow from the slab record; fix the tests first, including pass 44's coherence tests on Earth's plates;
name the slab data. No model code changed. A cloud pre-check of the flow on Earth's own mantle was run before fixing the
planet's bands, because pass 44's Earth test needed Earth numbers that did not exist yet.

**1. The choice: flow, not a kinematic kernel.** Journal 55's kernel ("negative, widening and weakening with depth")
would need a width and an amplitude per depth. That is exactly what a Stokes flow kernel is, and a hand-made one would
be fitted. The flow takes those from the viscosity profile (Earth measurement), costs little (below), and gives the
surface flow too, which pass 44 named as the missing mechanism of Earth's coherent plate motion. So D is the flow.

**2. The design** (values from Earth or mechanism; nothing fitted to the planet)
- **Slab record (mantle frame).** Each step, every trench node of the downgoing plate consumes area (closing rate × dt
  × line length, `BOUNDARY_NODE_H` h per node). The parcel carries the lithosphere's thermal mass excess. By isostasy
  this equals the floor's own subsidence: Δm = (ρ_m − ρ_w)(GDH1(age) − GDH1(0)) per m² (~6.9·10⁶ kg/m² at 80 Myr). That is
  the planet's own age–depth curve (`isostasy.gdh1_depth`), so no new constant. Upper mantle: the parcel descends along
  the mean deep dip, 58° (Lallemand et al. 2005, G-cubed 6, Q09006: 58 ± 14° below 125 km), at the closing rate, so it
  moves toward the overriding side by z / tan 58°. Lower mantle (> 660 km): it sinks vertically at 12 mm/yr (van der
  Meer et al. 2010, Nature Geosci. 3, 36; their 2018 atlas, Tectonophysics 723, 309: 10–40 mm/yr at the top of the lower
  mantle, 10–15 to ~1,700 km). It leaves the record at 2,850 km. Parcels merge by (cell, layer). The thermal-expansivity
  decrease with depth is not applied in the first build. It would weaken the lower-mantle loads, and it is the first
  mechanism to add if the amplitude band (P-d) fails high.
- **Flow.** Incompressible Stokes in a spherical shell with radial viscosity, solved per degree by propagator matrices
  (Hager & O'Connell 1981): dX/d ln r = A X with X = (u_r, u_h, r σ_rr/η₀, r σ_rθ/η₀). Each density sheet is a jump in
  σ_rr. Free slip at the surface and the CMB, uniform g 9.81, no self-gravitation (a ~10% effect at the lowest degrees;
  documented). Viscosity: the `sh08` profile (HC / SEATREE `visc.sh08`; 100 km lid 2.6·10²² Pa s, asthenosphere
  2–4·10²⁰, lower mantle to 1.1·10²⁴). Degrees 1–31, the Earth models' own truncation, so both bodies get the same
  filter. Only density below **z_cut = 300 km** drives it: below cratonic keels (~250 km), so Earth's fast anomalies
  there are slabs, and within the literature's 220–350 km cut (Steinberger et al. 2019, Tectonophysics 760, 221). The
  slab's top 300 km is the plate's own (slab pull and the trench are already modelled). Outputs per step: the dynamic
  topography h (air-loaded −σ_rr / (ρ_m g)). It is applied ×ρ_m/(ρ_m − ρ_w) where the node was under the sea last step,
  matching GDH1's water loading. The other output is the free-slip surface velocity v_fs.
- **On the planet.** `dyn_rule` 'slabs' replaces the random knot field. The hotspot swells and volcanism are unchanged.
  Journal 55's random short-wavelength part is dropped: Earth's slab proxy itself has P₃₀/P₂ = 6·10⁻⁴, and noise would
  be plate-blind again. `basal_traction` (torque law only): each plate feels C_b (1 + (keel − 1) c)(v_fs − v_plate).
  With v_plate = v_fs that is free slip, so the existing basal-drag coefficient carries it and there is **no new
  coefficient**.
- **Start and cost.** The record starts empty, so the first ~60 Myr are a transient (lower-mantle transit ~190 Myr).
  The tests read the last 100 Myr. Kernels: once per run (31 degrees × ~52 layers × a 4×4 propagator). Per step: one
  adjoint SHT of the parcels per layer, one scalar and one spin-1 synthesis at the nodes (ducc0), well under the step's
  other costs.

**3. Earth pre-check (cloud; `outputs/planet-calibration/precheck-p45-cloud/`, printout `reviews/earth_flow_traction.txt`)**
- Solver checks: the system's eigenvalues are l+1, l−1, −l, −l−2 (the Stokes solutions); the eigen-propagator matches
  RK4 to 3·10⁻¹² at l = 30. A dense sheet gives negative surface topography; the surface kernel tends to 1 for a load
  at the surface and to 0 at the CMB (isoviscous l = 2: 0.96 at 100 km, 0.40 at 1,500 km, 0.09 at 2,500 km), and falls
  faster with depth at high l. The spin-1 gradient synthesis matches finite differences to 1·10⁻⁹. The HC reference
  code (Becker) could not be compiled here (blocked), so the comparison with it is still owed. Its source was read
  only, and none of it is copied.
- Earth densities (SEATREE, below): Lithgow-Bertelloni & Richards' slab model `lrr98d` is not usable for amplitude.
  Its anomaly grows toward the CMB (rms 0.01% at 300 km, 0.49% at 2,850 km), and its excess mass below 300 km is
  2.9·10²⁰ kg against ~2·10²¹ from subduction rate × thermal mass. SMEAN2 and S40RTS × 0.2 (dlnρ/dlnVs), fast part
  only below 300 km: 1.37 / 1.33·10²¹ kg. That is the Earth slab proxy.

Earth's observed residual topography holds ~0.85 of its power in degrees 1–3 (Valentine & Davies 2020); the slab proxy
matches that shape. Its high-degree power is far below Earth's (P₃₀/P₂ ~0.03), which comes from above 300 km. Models
from all of the tomography overpredict degree 2 by more than 2× (Steinberger et al. 2019). Amplitudes are compared with
V&D's spectrum in their normalization in the build pass.

- With the coefficients held, traction alone moves C(d) by +0.11 … +0.13 (slab proxy) and +0.22 … +0.25 (all > 300 km),
  and raises T-a from 0.67 to 0.73–0.75. The refits barely move the coefficients (C_b 1.6–1.8·10¹⁵, keel ×3.9–4.9). F_sp
  sits on the box's upper edge in every fit, as in pass 43: a known T-e note, not new.
- Coherence scales with the flow's strength. Slabs alone lift Earth's plates from the null to ~0, about a third of
  Earth's excess. Earth's hot upwellings (all > 300 km) take it to +0.1. The rest needs the 100–300 km layer, where the
  thermal scaling also makes cratonic keels dense, which is not physical (isopycnic roots), so that row is not evidence.
- **No flow variant reaches Earth's slow continental rifts** (CRB 0.19–0.30 against 0.74) or CTF. The slow ocean–continent
  convergence (OCB) reaches Earth's only with the convergent-coupling term, which fails T-d by locking CCB / OCB, as in
  pass 43. Earth's CRB pairs (Nubia–Somalia, Eurasia–Amur, North America's interior rifts) are young, slow rifts across
  still-strong lithosphere. Their co-motion looks like a rift's resistance to opening (mechanical coupling across an
  incipient rift), not mantle flow. New open item; not part of D.

**4. Reading pass 44's Earth test (fixed 31 Oct)**: "on Earth's plates, with the law plus flow traction, C(d) of different
plates > 0 at < 2,000 km, and ρ < 0.5 in Bird's slow classes toward Earth's (CRB 0.74, OCB 0.69)".
- With the slab proxy (what the planet's record represents) it fails, at the edge for C(d) (−0.04 … +0.01) and clearly
  for the slow classes (unchanged). With all of the tomography below 300 km C(d) passes (+0.06 … +0.11); the slow classes
  still fail.
- So traction from slabs is not the mechanism of Earth's slow boundaries, and it moves coherence only partway.
  Consequences: D is built for the dynamic topography, by its own tests (section 5). `basal_traction` is built with it
  (no new coefficient, a few lines). By the 30 Oct rule it is swept only if a paired probe on the planet's same states
  passes P-h. T2 is not expected to move through traction.

**5. Tests fixed before building**
- **Unit (U):** U1 eigen-propagator vs RK4 ≤ 1e-9 at l ≤ 31; U2 kernel limits (surface → 1, CMB → 0, sign, high-l decay);
  U3 spin-1 gradient vs finite differences ≤ 1e-6; U4 the record conserves mass (deposited = in the record + left at the
  CMB) and sinks at the set rates; U5 `dyn_rule` 'random' (default) stays identical step by step (n = 48, ~70 steps,
  threads 1); U6 the Earth pre-check numbers reproduce from `data/mantle/` (rms 222 m, flow 1.35 cm/yr ± 1%).
- **Earth (E), formalised as `planet_reviews.mantle_flow_earth`:** E1 the slab proxy's spectrum and amplitude above,
  plus V&D's observed spectrum in their normalization; E2 the traction table above. These are reference numbers, not
  bands.
- **Planet (P), six seeds, last 100 Myr, `dyn_rule` slabs vs the control:**
  - P-a share of the field's variance in degrees 1–3 ≥ 0.6 (Earth slab proxy 0.82–0.84; observed ~0.85); P₃₀/P₂ ≤ 0.1.
  - P-b lows over slabs: area-weighted correlation of the field with distance to the record's parcels younger than
    100 Myr > 0.
  - P-c rate at fixed points (mantle frame): median ≤ 20 m/Myr (Rubey et al. 2017).
  - P-d amplitude: rms of the field (air-loaded) within 0.5–2× the Earth slab proxy (110–460 m); its 10-Myr series over
    the last 100 Myr has no step > 20% (no pulsing).
  - P-e surface flow rms within 0.5–2× the proxy (0.7–2.7 cm/yr).
  - P-f gates and guards as for every sweep: elevation gates ±300 m on 6 of 6 seeds, plate count (watch > 40), ledgers.
    Land fraction, drowned continental crust, land p50 and texture are reported, not scored (they were tuned with the
    random field).
  - P-g (read, not scored): C(d), ρ by class, T2, io trench age, the record's mass and depth profile against Earth's.
  - P-h traction, paired probe (cloud, seed 1001, same states, 25–30 Myr, torque law): C(d) < 2,000 km rises
    ≥ +0.06 (half the Earth proxy's +0.12) without T2 rising > 2k km or the plate count jumping. Only then a sweep arm.
- **Rule:** adopt `dyn_rule` slabs if P-a … P-e pass on the six-seed mean and P-f holds on 6 of 6 seeds. If P-d fails
  high, add α(z) from mineral physics (a mechanism), never a scale factor. If P-b fails, the record or the kernel is wrong:
  debug, do not tune.
