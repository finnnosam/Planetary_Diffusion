  It lies in broad pieces: depth from its edge p50 / p90 295 / 757 km (Earth 92 / 322), largest piece 29.5 Mkm²
  (Earth 7.9); land above 2 km has slope p50 2.3 m/km (Earth 11.1). > 45 km land stands at 2,845 m (Earth 1,234).
- Area ledger over 250 Myr (share of the surface): collision −0.111, rift +0.067, arcs +0.000, net −0.049.
- History: the > 45 km share rises at every 10 Myr step, 0.045 (start) → 0.150 (−200) → 0.236 (−100) → 0.320

  median 47–52 km throughout; continental area 0.383 → 0.308; crust volume −14%. Thick crust is never removed: the
  sinks (erosion of low-slope plateaus, the collapse flow, which spreads but conserves) are slower than collision

**8. Next mechanism, R: loss of the eclogitised lower crust** (to be built in pass 54; the test set is fixed there,
before any run). Why R and not the others on item 1's list:
- Thick crust is built by collision and nothing removes it (section 6). R removes thickness where it is built, as on
  Earth (Clift et al. 2009: 1.1 km³/yr by delamination; Krystopowicz & Currie 2013: an eclogitised root founders
  within ~3–15 Myr of forming, given a weak, hot lower crust; Central Andes 50–70 km felsic crust that lost its
  mafic lower part).
- Extension (E–E4) failed four times (journals 44–50). Area loss at collisions (0.43 km³/yr on Earth) reduces how
  much area becomes thickness but deepens the area deficit.
- The values come from measurement and mechanism: the depth at which mafic lower crust becomes eclogite (phase
  equilibria), the mafic lower crust's share of the column (Rudnick & Gao 2003), the foundering time (numerical and
  geological estimates). None is fitted to the planet. To source in pass 54.
- Its cost: it removes volume (~1 km³/yr on Earth, ~3.5% over 250 Myr), on a planet already short of volume. The
  volume source is B-d2's arc addition (pass 41 arm C moved volume and water toward Earth). The pass-54 sweep is
  therefore planned as control / R / R + B-d2 arm C's settings; the arm list is fixed with the tests.
- Tests to fix in pass 54 from Earth data: > 45 km and > 55 km shares (0.086 / 0.018), the E tests already in
  `interior_probe` (slope of land > 2 km, > 45 km depth from its edge), the coast-distance rise and lowland share
  (section 4, new), R's flux against Earth's ~1.1 km³/yr, and the guards (elevation gates, margins, T2–T5).
- What R cannot do: the collapse flow (`crust_kappa_m2_yr` and its e-fold, a Tibet-tuned M-target) still spreads
  whatever thick crust exists into plateaus; C (Earth's cold, thick lithosphere stands ~280 m lower) is not in the
  model. Both stay on item 1's list.
