**1. Earth's spectrum, from the coefficients.**
- The files hold, per real orthonormal coefficient (Dahlen & Tromp's, as `real_sph_harm`), the posterior mean y and its
  standard error s (water-loaded, km). The expected power of the true field per degree is Σ_m (y² + s²); the mean model
  alone (Σ y²) is shrunk toward zero where there are no data (the continents) and is not the spectrum.
- High-accuracy spot data (the paper's headline set), expected power (km²): l 1 0.21, **l 2 0.62**, l 3 0.39, l 4 0.31,
  l 5 0.48, l 10 0.15, l 15 0.07, l 20 0.049, **l 30 0.022**. Diagonal resampling gives l 2's 50% range 0.475–0.744 (the
  paper: "likely 0.46–0.76") and l 30's 0.019–0.024 ("around 0.02"): the published numbers come from these files.
- Shares of the power to l 30: **l 1–3 0.287**, l 4–8 0.386, l 9–30 0.327; l 1–15 **0.860**. Total 4.25 km²: rms
  √(P/4π) **0.58 km water-loaded, 0.41 km air-loaded** (× 2,300 / 3,300, the measurement's own densities); l 1–3 alone
  0.31 / 0.22 km. P30 / P2 0.035. The other two data sets: all spot 0.29 / 0.84 / 0.59 km, spot + ship 0.29 / 0.84 /
  0.60 km.
- **The "~85% in degrees 1–3".** `plotSpectra` computes the printed "Proportion in degree 1-3" as
  `random_spot_ha[:,0:15].sum(1) / random_spot_ha.sum(1)` on the per-degree power array (`modelToPower` returns one column
  per degree). `0:15` is the number of *coefficients* at l 1–3 (3 + 5 + 7), used as a slice of *degrees*: it is the share
  of degrees 1–15. Recomputed: 0.860 (sampled 0.859 ± 0.015), the paper's ~85–86%. The same slice is used correctly a
  few lines below on the coefficient array (the l 1–3 maps). I read this as a slip in the paper's script; the
  per-degree values the paper states (peak, l 30, an order of magnitude) are not affected. Worth telling the authors;
  that is the user's call, not done here.
- It agrees with the two other estimates in the project: Holdt et al. 2022's own l 1–30 fit (pass 52) 0.473 km rms with
  22% at l 1–3; Davies et al. 2019's ARD spectrum (in the same repository, `Davies_ARD.dat`) peaks at l 2 (0.46 km²) and
  is still 0.11 km² at l 10. All three put most of the power above l 3.
- **What it corrects in the project's reading** (passes 47–57 used 86% and 0.22 km air-loaded as Earth's whole signal):
  - Today's random field (applied, not loaded: rms 442 m, 0.12 at l 1–3; pass 47) has about Earth's amplitude (Earth
    405 m air-loaded, 580 m water-loaded), not 1.4–2× it. Its shape is still wrong: flat power to l 30 (P30 / P2 ~1
    against 0.035; l 1–3 0.12 against 0.29).
  - D (parked): its six-seed flow (727 m air-loaded) is 1.8× Earth's whole signal, not 3.3×; E3 (Earth's own subduction
    through D, 516–645 m) 1.3–1.6×. But D's power is at l 1–3 (E3: 84% of it, pass 52), where Earth's is unchanged at ~0.22 km
    air-loaded, so pass 52's per-degree reading stands (E3's l 1–3 is 2.7× Earth's there). D stays parked; nothing in
    journal 74's order changes. P-d's band (110–460 m) was set around the slab proxy and is not reopened.
  - Journal 55's "slope ~3 gives 88% in l 1–3" plan and journal 74's "86%" are superseded by the measured table itself:
    nothing is fitted to a share.

**2. The build** (`planet_tectonics.mantle`, `planet_grid.fields.spectrum_field`, `sim`, `params`).
- `dyn_rule` 'measured': knot fields every `dt_knot_myr` (40, unchanged) drawn as Gaussian isotropic fields with
  `VD2020_POWER_KM2` (the 30 expected powers above) per degree, each coefficient of variance p_l / (2l + 1); not
  rescaled (one realization's rms scatters 360–447 m, p5–p95). No `dyn_std_m`, no slope, no fitted number.
- Loading: the measurement is water-loaded (Δρ 2,300); the field is held air-loaded (× 2,300 / 3,300) and multiplied by
  ρ_m / (ρ_m − ρ_w) (3,300 / 2,270) on the nodes under the sea at the last step, as D's flow is. On wet nodes it is
  Earth's water-loaded field × 1.013 (the planet's water density 1,030 against the measurement's 1,000).
- Blending: cos a · K_k + sin a · K_k+1, a = (π/2) s(f), s the smoothstep: variance constant, rate zero at the knots.
  The random rule's linear blend pulses 360–514 m on a 40 Myr cycle (journal 55).
- Its own random stream (`default_rng([seed, 8])`); the random knots are still drawn, so the hotspots take the same
  numbers in every rule (tested). Hotspot swells are added unloaded, as before; by their size and count (~6–10 swells of
  800 m at σ 500 km) they add ≤ 5% of Earth's power at l 10–20, inside the measurement's spread, so they stay.
- Snapshots of 'measured' runs carry `dynamic_topography_air_m` (air-loaded, with swells) as D's do.
- Generator alone (n = 48, 300 realizations): rms 403 ± 26 m (target 405), l 1–3 share 0.27 ± 0.07, P30 / P2 median
  0.044; rate at fixed points, median |dh/dt| 10.7 m/Myr (1 Myr) and 10.9 (5 Myr) against Rubey et al. 2017's ≤ 20.
- Default path: identical (n = 48, 70 steps, `threads: 1`, old and new code, every state field equal).
- Tests: `tests/test_dyn_measured.py` (new): M1 the table recomputed from the coefficient file (and the paper's own
  statements, including 0.86 at l 1–15); M2 `spectrum_field`'s power (one degree, and the whole table: 405.4 m);
  M3 the constant-variance blend, the water factor and the hotspots' stream; M4 PlateSim with 'measured', the air
  field in the snapshot, the water factor on the wet nodes, 'random' still the default.
- The planet-layer test subset with the new file, in the cloud: 109 passed, 5 skipped (data/mantle's tomography and
  viscosity are not there). The sweep's `.cmd` runs it on Windows first.

Reading:
- The build is the measurement: every M check passes on the planet, and the rms series no longer pulses (0.04 against
  0.26). The water loading lifts the applied rms to 541 m (Earth's water-loaded 580 m on the ocean).
- Gates and plates hold. Export, volume, area, drowned crust and sea level all moved toward Earth on this seed, by
  amounts comparable with the chaos between arms (journal 79: ±~250 m on the interior residual), so they are read on
  six seeds, not here.
- **The interior lift does not go away** with Earth's spectrum: +161 m (control +146). It is not the blobs. With any
  plate-blind field, land that stands high enough to be far from the coast sits on the field's highs (a selection: a
  continent's interior is land because the field lifts it). On Earth the long-wavelength field follows subduction
  history (lows over slabs, as under southern Eurasia; highs far from them, as under Africa; Rubey et al. 2017), not the
  coastlines, and only a plate-aware field (D) can give that. So the measured field removes a
  fitted value but not the confound; C1 is read with and without the field again in the sweep, and the plate-aware
  pattern is a known, documented limit of the plate-blind field (journal 74 item 2).

**5. The Measured Dynamic Topography Sweep** (`Measured Dynamic Topography Sweep.cmd`; rule in its header, fixed 1 Nov
before the runs). Four arms × six seeds: combo-01 the no-flow base (= combo-01 of `sweep-20261101-165624`, identity);
combo-02 the base + measured; combo-03 combo-02 + C; combo-04 today's PARAMS + measured (control: combo-01 of
`sweep-20261101-115244`, not rerun).
- Part 1, the field (combo-04 for the default): M-a … M-e pass, and the integrity holds (pytest, gates 6 of 6, plates
  ≤ 40 on every seed, ledgers) → PARAMS `dyn_rule` 'measured'. No terrain band is asked of it: it replaces a fitted
  value with a measurement, and the realism rule keeps no fitted value for a band. Export, volume, margins and the
  hypsometry are read against the control; a guard the control holds and this arm fails is named as what the fitted
  field was carrying. Build checks fail → fix, not adopt. A gate fails → not adopted; where the field moves the gate
  is next.
- Part 2, C (combo-03 against combo-02): adopted only with Part 1 and C1 (corrected: −262 … −786 m), C2 (rise ≤ 772 m),
  ≥ 3 of P1–P4, C's term guard (−119 … −357 m) and every guard (gates, volume ≥ −14%, export 4–8, margins, plates).
  Not adopted → export (the narrow belts) next in every branch; C1 sets its base (passes → combo-03; fails → combo-02,
  with the interior's remaining excess carried as the residual).
- Why this order of tests: a measured value is adopted on whether it is the measurement and whether the model still
  works with it; whole-surface bands judge mechanisms that are supposed to produce them (C here), not inputs.
