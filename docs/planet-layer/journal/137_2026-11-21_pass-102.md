#### 5. Step-back on §0 item 3 (told to the user in the reply)

The order stands; item 3's next step is re-aimed by the finding above (a finding that the cause lies elsewhere becomes
the next arm). Both of the target's remaining failures, the drowned normal crust (2.6% of the surface against
Earth's 0.25%) and the high middle of the land with its high interiors, are one covariance: the continents' elevation
with the dynamic field. The planet's field acts on the continents at the full oceanic spectrum and the continents
select it: crust on a low drowns, crust on a high stays land. Earth's continents (as far as Holdt's continental
interpolation shows) sit on lows inland and on a field near zero at the drowned edges. So the next item is **how the
dynamic field meets the continents**. Candidates, each from a measurement or a mechanism, to be chosen and built in
pass 103:

- (a) **Earth's degree correlation of its residual topography with the continents.** Pass 101's method applied to the
  plate-blind rest: measure r_l between VD2020's field and Earth's continent function (crust ≥ 30 km on the grid), check
  it against Holdt 2022's independent grid as pass 101 did for the harmonics' reading, and draw the rest with that
  correlation to the planet's own continents (r_l may be negative: Earth's continents on lows). Caveat: VD2020's
  continental coefficients are extrapolated from oceanic spots; only degrees the oceans constrain carry meaning.
- (b) **The field under thick lithosphere** (a mechanism): the field's shallow sources cannot lie inside a keel. Needs
  a depth for the plate-blind part, which has none; the placed part has the kernels.
- Not: scaling the field's amplitude to a band (§0 Not to do); adding sediment to the drowned continent as if starved
  (pass 97–98); undoing the placed field.

Also open, read beside it: the middle of the land (500–1,000 m about the sea 8.1% against 5.6), C1 (−65 m against
−524), the young floor (0.391 against 0.30).
