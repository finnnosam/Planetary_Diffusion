**2. Which sediment the start should carry: measured.** The start's crust is already Earth's: `start_crust` 'earth'
quantile-maps the supercontinent onto CRUST1.0's crystalline thickness of Precambrian and platform crust > 500 km from
Bird's boundaries (`EARTH_STABLE_CRUST_KM`). The cover that same domain carries is the start's cover, by the same
reasoning: a supercontinent's interior is old stable crust, and Earth's stable crust in steady state carries this cover.
Measured on the n = 217 graph (`precheck-p89-startsed/earth_start_sed.py`):

The stable domain's cover is lighter than the whole continent's (1,331 against 1,633 m): it leaves out the passive
margins' wedges, which on Earth are mostly younger than Pangea's breakup. Not used: an age cut of Earth's cover (older
than 250 Myr) — no measurement of its volume by age on the grid is in hand, and the stable domain's cover is the
steady state of the crust the start already is.

The spatial structure is measured too (`sed_vario.py`): the correlation of the cover's normal scores with distance is
0.84 / 0.70 / 0.50 / 0.34 / 0.21 / 0.14 / 0.08 at 0–100 / –200 / –400 / –600 / –1,000 / –1,500 / –2,500 km. Of the
random fields `fields.random_field(lmax, slope)` tried (lmax 24, 60, 100 × slope 0.5, 1, 1.5, 2; six draws each), (100,
1.5) matches it (sum of squared misfit over the seven bins 0.017; the start crust's own field (24, 2) gives 1.98, its
basins far too broad). This sets a start condition's statistics to Earth's measured ones, as the quantile map does; no
planet output is used.
