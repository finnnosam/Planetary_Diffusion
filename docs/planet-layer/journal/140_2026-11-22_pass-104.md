The device shell (Linux VM, 2 cores, ducc0 0.42.0) reproduces it with HEAD against HEAD (`rep.py`: md5 of every
`state()` array per step, n = 48, seed 1001, two runs side by side): at `threads` 2, **7 of 41 states identical, the
first difference at step 7**, in `flow_topography_alm_m` / `flow_velocity_alm_m_yr` / `flow_power_raw_m2`, the same
step as on Windows. `which.py` repeats every call of the flow's transforms on its own input: `point_alm` (ducc0's
`adjoint_synthesis_general`, the slab parcels to coefficients) did not repeat on 4 of 44 calls; `synthesis` and
`gradient` 0 of 12. The flexure's adjoint (lmax 432 / 480) repeated on 42 of 42 calls in a 10-step run, and synthetic
clustered points repeated on 60 of 60 at both thread counts (`det2.py`): the race is intermittent and shows on the
model's own parcels. The adjoint spreads points onto a grid from several threads, and the order of their sums follows
the threads' timing; the synthesis computes each point on its own and repeats.

So the premise of the borrowed control (journal 135: "runs on the user's machine are deterministic") held at
`threads` 1 only, and pass 102's reading of its own failed borrow (journal 137 §1: the reference predated a67e919) was
probably this too (first difference at −242 Myr). The two trajectories of one model that pass 102 used as the chaos
range remain valid as such.

**Fixed** (no model change): the adjoint runs on one thread in `mantle_flow.point_alm` and in
`SphereFlexure.deflection`; the synthesis keeps its threads. On a patched copy: `threads` 2 against itself 41 of 41
(seed 1001, 40 steps) and 71 of 71 (seed 1002, 70 steps); `threads` 1 against HEAD 71 of 71 (seed 1001, 70 steps: the
arithmetic at `threads` 1 is unchanged). Cost: ~5 ms per adjoint call (11 against 6 ms at lmax 480, 240k points).
The fix changes the arithmetic at `threads` 2, so the sweeps' trajectories change once more: the next sweep cannot
borrow from `sweep-20261121-220857`. `Repeatable Control.cmd` runs today's model on six seeds as the new reference and
re-runs seed 1001 to test repeatability on Windows (§5).
