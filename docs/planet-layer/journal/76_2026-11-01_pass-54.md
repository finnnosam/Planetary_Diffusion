**2. Where R acts.** The density of mafic crust crosses the mantle's only in the eclogite facies proper: Chapman et
al. (2019) put the threshold for mafic crust at ~60–70 km / 1.8–2.2 GPa (neutral buoyancy at ~65 km, 2 GPa, under
warm conditions; written for subducted MORB). Krystopowicz & Currie (2013) model foundering only once thickening
carries the lower crust into the eclogite field, and remove it within ~3–15 Myr (eclogite +130 kg/m³, weak lower
crust). Granulite stays metastable without fluids (Urals root after ~250 Myr). So R removes crust below ~55–70 km.
The planet's excess is mostly at 45–55 km (0.24 of the continent against Earth's 0.068; > 55 km 0.09 against
0.018). R alone would leave the pile and the plateaus. Not built this pass; it returns as the cap (section 4).

Reading: the collapse flow (`crust_kappa_m2_yr` 1000 × (e^((T−45 km)/6 km) − 1), to 200×; tuned to hold Tibet-like
plateaus, an M-target) makes the plateaus. It spreads collided crust until it stalls just above 45 km, over hundreds
of km at low slope, where erosion is slow. Without it, belts stay narrow and steep and erosion removes them: four of
the item-1 shape tests move onto Earth's values. The cost is volume: what erodes is not replaced, because the
planet's crust addition (arcs 0.55, volcanics 0.07 km³/yr) is a fifth of Earth's arc addition (3.8 km³/yr, Clift
et al. 2009), and its sediment subduction (4.4) is ~3× Earth's. The thickest columns reach 100–120 km for a moment
without the flow: that is where R belongs (section 2), on 0.2% of the continent. The domed interior is reduced, not
removed (rise 825 m against 386): the rest is sea level (290 m below the datum) and C.

**4. Design decision** (Claude's, by the goal and the rules; written here, not brought to the user: reversible,
inside item 1's agreed direction).
- The collapse flow is a fitted value that makes the bands fail. The physics it stood for is real: hot thickened
  crust flows (Tibet's channel at ~10¹⁸–10²⁰ Pa s, Clark & Royden 2000, from memory as in journal 42; to confirm if the law is built). But the fitted form never cools. It lets
  old thick crust flow as if it were hot, for 250 Myr. The first test is the simplest realistic one: the hot-crust
  term off, the measured cold flow kept. If young plateaus are then missing, it returns as a measured channel law
  with a cooling clock (thickened crust heats and weakens for ~10–30 Myr and cools over ~100 Myr; England & Thompson
  1984; numbers to source then).
- Volume: B-d2 arm C (`trench_polarity` pair, `trench_flux` consumed, `arc_H_ocean_m` 1,000, `arc_accretion` True;
  pass 41) moved volume and water toward Earth. It was rejected then on T2–T4, > 45 km and margins; its > 45 km
  failure came with the collapse flow on. It is the volume arm.
- R (the cap at the eclogite depth) is built after the sweep if the no-flow arms show the > 65 km columns or the
  > 55 km share above Earth's.
- Dynamic topography's spectrum follows item 1 (user, 1 Nov).
