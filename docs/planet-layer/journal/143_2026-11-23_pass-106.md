#### 3. What the arm can and cannot be expected to do

The surface the model ends each step with is the eroded surface, which repairs ~2/3 of what the composition adds, and
that repair costs erosion work every step (the eroded stage makes 629 one-cell features while it removes 743). A
conservative transfer removes the per-step source. It does not remove what the smoothing left untouched: with every
plate's crust and sediment smooth, the first surface (`smoothed`, −19 Myr) already had 313 holes, 309 islands and 45.9
crossings, and 250 / 342 / 44.4 after one eroded step. That floor comes from what was not smoothed (the lithosphere's
thermal age, the ocean floor, the field, the flexure, the column water-load decision, sharp continental edges) and from
erosion itself. So the arm can be expected to move the end of a step from ~416 holes and ~54 crossings toward ~250–310
and ~44–46, and the low flat interior from 5.1% toward ~6.5% of land (Earth 13, 19.1 and 14.2%). The frozen prediction
for the confirmation read is written when the arm is built (pass 107), from this trace and the arm's own pre-check.
The floor is the next question after the arm: a second trace that also smooths the thermal age, and splits the column
water-load decision and the flexure, before anything is built for it.
