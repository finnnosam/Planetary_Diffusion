#### 4. (b) as a mechanism: the old cover is rock

Where the cover goes is set by what can move it. Under `sediment_entrainment` 'cover' (pass 86) the whole sediment
layer is loose, entrained without bedrock's threshold down to its base: fresh alluvium, but also the start's
continental cover, which is Earth's stable platform cover (CRUST1.0 on Precambrian / platform crust; journal 115),
i.e. Palaeozoic–Mesozoic sedimentary rock. The seed-1018 pre-check saw it leave the high land within ~40 Myr and
settle low (journal 116); the planet's cover on 40–55 km crust is 0.3× Earth's and on 20–30 km 1.3–1.8×. On Earth
sediment buried below ~2–3 km is rock: compaction is mechanical down to 2–3 km (> 70–100 °C), chemical below
(Bjørlykke 1999), and quartz cementation starts at 70–80 °C (Walderhaug 1994; Bjørlykke & Egeberg 1993). Rock
erodes as rock (Earth's ¹⁰Be-calibrated K and θ hold for it as for crystalline basement at this grid: assumed, no new
number; Moosdorf et al. 2018's lithological erodibility index would be a later refinement).

This is the cover's place by routing (§0's framing of (b)), not its density or compaction (measured laws). It is
not a constant lift and fits nothing to elevation.

#### 5. Built: `sediment_rock`

`params.py`: `sediment_rock` 'none' (as built, default) / 'burial'; `rock_depth_m` 2,000 (bulk; 70 °C at Earth's
mean sedimentary-basin gradient ~30 °C/km from a 10 °C surface; onset measured, gradient assumed).

- Each plate carries `rock` (solid m, float32), carried with the other fields through composition, new nodes,
  holes, splits, merges, piece transfers (stacked like the sediment) and back-arc strips; the world's `rock` is
  clipped to its sediment when composed.
- `surface._erode` under 'burial': the cover is the loose part (sediment − rock); within each substep a cut below the
  loose cover takes the rock next (`rock -= max(−(cover + dz), 0)`), so rock is lost only by real cuts.
- `sim.step` after the sediment's update: rock = min(rock left by the cuts, sediment), then max(·, sediment − the
  solid in the top `rock_depth_m` of bulk) (`isostasy.loose_cap`, the continental or oceanic Athy law; ~1.22 km of
  solid in 2 km of bulk on both); the change goes back to the plates as the sediment's does.
- `start.py`: the start's continental cover is rock (all of it); the start floor below the cap.
- Snapshot `sediment_rock_m` when the rule is on. 'burial' needs `sediment_entrainment` 'cover' (raises otherwise).
