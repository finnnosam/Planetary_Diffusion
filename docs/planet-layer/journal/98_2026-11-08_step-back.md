### Step-back (8 Nov, after pass 74, before the Pelagic Sweep is run): what the land's height failure is made of. Half of it is the column (how high a column of a given crust stands), present from the start before any plate moves; a third is the low sea; a sixth is the extra thick crust. The rift-line thread acted on that last sixth, and only as a side effect; the pelagic arm acts on part of the sea. The column has never been an arm or a scored line. **2. What "the column" is.** Elevation by crust + sediment thickness, on the datum (0 Myr) against Earth's, relative
to its sea:

| T (km) | 25–30 | 30–35 | 35–40 | 40–45 | 45–55 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| planet, on the datum | −466 | −149 | 369 | 1,042 | 1,994 |
| planet, lithosphere > 1 Gyr, on the datum | −1,717 | −496 | 112 | 755 | 1,619 |
| Earth (all / outside orogens) | −72 / −76 | 98 / 92 | 321 / 306 | 359 / 329 | 885 / 431 (45–50) |

The planet's land rises ~120 m per km of crust from 30 to 45 km (uniform `rho_crust` 2,830: Airy's 142 m/km; C moves
columns by age, not by thickness: old-lithosphere columns are as steep). Earth's rises ~25 m/km: its residual against
its own crust's Airy (`earth-lithosphere-residual-n217.json`, pass 57) falls ~100 m per km of crust (−544 m at 20–25 km
to −3,064 at 45–50), inside and outside orogens. Caveat: thickness bins carry CRUST1.0's errors (regression dilution;
decision 1 Nov). The spreads do not depend on binning: on Earth's own thickness distribution the planet's land is
1.35× as spread as Earth's (1.37× at the start), and the interior read by distance inland says the same (z − Airy
+91 m against Earth's −367 m at 40 km crust). Lamb et al. (2020, whole-lithosphere isostasy) find continental
elevation is set by lithosphere thickness as much as by crust, and by density variations of both.

Consequence: every change in how much thick crust the tectonics make is amplified ~4× into land height against
Earth. That is why the split geometry swings the land both ways (chord −145, force +215 m): it changes the collision
total, and the column turns that into height.

**3. The sea** (−564 m on the datum). The floor on its own age stands deeper on the datum than Earth's below its
sea: 2–10 Myr −3,393 against −3,328; 30–60 −4,807 / −4,525; 60–100 −5,346 / −4,930; 100–200 −5,538 / −4,832 (the
planet's floor is bare and keeps sinking; Earth's ETOPO floor carries its sediment and flattens). Rough budget: that
excess depth ~60% of the low sea, the continental-area deficit (0.350 against 0.384) ~40%. The Pelagic Sweep (built,
to run) acts on the first; its expectation (~100 m) is a third of the sea's share.
