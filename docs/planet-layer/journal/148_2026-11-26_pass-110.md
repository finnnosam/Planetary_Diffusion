#### 4. The arm built: `rift_area` (PARAMS; default 'world')

- 'world' (as built): the kernel strains the zone by each gap's world cell area.
- 'plates': the strain is the area the owning plate's raster switches on over each gap this step (`_opened_area`: the
  unmasked nodes of the owning plate whose forward nearest node is new crust, exactly the set `_update_rasters` switches
  on; the rotations and the ownership are already this step's when `_rift` runs). The zone then gives exactly the volume
  the new plate nodes take. A gap no plate opens over takes no volume and the world shows it as the zone's crust (Tbar,
  before breakup), carried by no plate (the return skips new crust), so the world view does not gain one-cell water
  holes; counted in `erosion_totals['rift_unopened_nodes']`. The rift's area ledger and `rift_stretched_m3` count the
  opened area.
- No number added. The breakup test reads the gap crust of the opened area (a gap that opens more than its cell is a
  larger finite stretch).
- Tests (`tests/test_rift_area.py`, 4 of 4; added to PLANET_TESTS): the kernel with the cell's own area is the old
  kernel bit for bit; with other opened areas it conserves exactly (the zone's loss = Σ Tg × opened) and a gap opening
  nothing takes nothing; `_opened_area` at rift time equals the area the update switches on over new crust (holes filled
  afterwards only add); in a run under 'plates' the zone's loss equals the new nodes' rift crust at every rifting step.
  With them: `test_plates` 66 of 66, `test_remap_tent` and `test_return_transfer` 26 of 26 (cloud).
