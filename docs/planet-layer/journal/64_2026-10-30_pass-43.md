**1. The law (mechanism; Forsyth & Uyeda 1975, Geophys. J. R. astr. Soc. 43).** Each plate's angular velocity w_i
solves the torque balance of all plates at once (no inertia):

  F_sp S_i + F_rp P_i + F_su U_i = [C_b (D_i + (keel − 1) K_i) + C_s Z_i] w_i + C_c Σ_j X_ij (w_i − w_j)

- slab pull S at the downgoing plate's trench (normal out of the plate), ridge push P at oceanic ridges (into the
  plate), slab suction U on the overriding plate;
- basal drag D = R² Σ a (I − x xᵀ) (a Couette asthenosphere: traction −C_b v), larger under continental keels (K);
- slab resistance Z = Σ L R² q qᵀ, q = x × n (drag on the slab, linear in the plate's velocity toward its trench; with
  slab pull it sets a terminal speed F_sp / C_s independent of plate size, which is why Cocos is as fast as the
  Pacific);
- coupling X at convergence without a slab (Bird's CCB and OCB; the planet's COLLISION and CONVERGENT).

**7. Where Earth's slow convergence lies** (Bird's steps by plate pair): OCB 17.4k km, 66% of it touching a plate
under 10 Mkm²; CCB 23.0k, 68%; SUB 51.5k, 77%. Slow convergence is not a microplate boundary in particular. Its
longest pieces lie between plates that move almost together: AU–IN 2.3k (the diffuse Indo-Australian boundary), AF–SO
1.7k, SA–SC 1.2k, AF–EU 1.0k, BS–TI 1.0k. The planet has no pairs like that: its plates are cut by rifts and
subduction-initiation events that give the two parts different motions from the start, and each part then follows
its own forces.

**Decision (Claude, by the goal and the 30 Oct rule; not a user decision): no sweep.** `Torque Law Sweep.cmd` was
drafted with its rule (arms: control, torque, torque + pair + slab gate) but not placed. `force_law` stays 'lengths';
the code, its Earth test and the probes stay. The law is Earth's mechanism with Earth-fitted values; it should return
when something gives it slow ocean–ocean convergence to keep slow. By the paired probes neither the torque law
(+0.3k) nor torque + gate (−1.1k) comes near T2's −14k, and a six-seed sweep would be decided by the trajectories'
chaotic swing (±7k km within 30 Myr here). The fragmentation of the one long cloud run is a watch-point for any
future sweep with the torque law (no detach events in the 30 Myr probes).

**What T2 needs, restated.** Three passes have now moved levers that act on rates (pass 41 polarity, pass 42 the slab
gate, pass 43 the force law); none moves the length. The planet's ocean–ocean convergence has Earth's total length
(38.5k vs SUB + OCB 38.2k) but all of it is fast and slab-bearing. Earth's slow part lies between plates moving
nearly together. Next pass: one diagnostic of the geometry before any new candidate. Compare, plate pair by plate
pair at 0 Myr, the planet's intra-oceanic trench with Earth's (which pairs, their relative speed, their age, whether
one side is a rift child). If no mechanism shows, T2 is set aside and the work moves to D (dynamic topography from
the slabs), the next item in order.
