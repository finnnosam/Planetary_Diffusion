#### 3. Step-back (§0): the field's placement un-parked

§0's order put the drowned crust's source next, then the high side, with the field's placement on the not-to-do list
(D parked, journal 74: "Earth's continents sit on its lows, so placement lowers them"). The reads above move the target's
remaining spread onto the field: the thin crust is at Earth's size, the high side and the low tail both carry the
field's ±150 m, and with the field out the continents match. Taking the field's placement off the not-to-do list is a
change of the order agreed with the user on 31 Oct, so it went to the user: **"Un-park the field" (user, 21 Nov)**.

The parked reason becomes a prediction for the sweep to test, not a reason to skip it: placement by the plates may
lower the continents' level (Earth's continents sit on its lows) while it narrows their spread. D's amplitude question
(the slab flow 3.3× Earth's observed residual topography, Hoggard et al. 2016's known gap) stays out of the way if the
arm takes the pattern from the plates and the amplitude from the measurement.

slab-history flow (`dyn_rule` 'slabs', built 31 Oct) for the pattern, rescaled degree by degree to the measured power
