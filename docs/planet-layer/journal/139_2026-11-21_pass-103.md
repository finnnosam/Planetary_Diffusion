Readings:
- **Candidate (a) has nothing to impose.** At l ≥ 4, Earth's measured correlation is zero where the oceans constrain
  it, and B's plate-blind rest already gives zero (−0.04; A's slab-placed l 4–10 gave −0.22, the trench lows under the
  continents' rims). The drowned normal crust on the rest's lows (journal 137 §4) is selection by elevation among
  uncorrelated lows; Earth's l 4–10 on its continents is not measured.
- **At l 1–3 the planet's sign is the opposite of Earth's.** These degrees are the slab record's (placed share 0.99 /
  0.78 / 0.10; Earth's own slabs explain Earth's l 1–2, journal 136), so the gap is geography: the planet's continents
  stay in one cap (§1) with its slab girdle round it, and the girdle's flow puts the cap on its interior high (r_1
  +0.6–0.7; the interiors on +364 m). Earth's dispersed continents sit astride the circum-Pacific girdle's degree-2 low.
- (b), the field under thick lithosphere, needs a depth for the plate-blind part, which has none; and at l ≥ 4 there is
  no measured continental signal to move toward.

So §0 item 3's remaining gap at the degrees Earth constrains is where the continents are, not how the field is drawn,
and the field reaches the continents' arrangement only through the plates. The flow that puts a clustered continent
on a high is the return flow of its slab girdle, the mechanism that breaks supercontinents on Earth (Gurnis 1988,
Nature 332, 695; Zhong et al. 2007, EPSL 261, 551). In the model it pushes the surface up and pulls on nothing.

#### 4. Built: `rift_traction` (PARAMS, default False)

- `params.py`: `rift_traction` False (as built) / True: `rift_line` 'force''s torques (`_cut_forces`) add the basal
  traction of the planet's own flow on the plate, C_b (1 + (keel − 1) c) a (x × v_flow), the term `basal_traction` books in
  the torque law, with the existing `basal_drag_pa_s_m` and `keel_drag_factor`. The ranking of the cuts, the tension
  test and the cut chosen change; the split rate (Earth's rifted production / L) and the plate motions (`force_law`
  'lengths') do not. No number added. Needs `dyn_rule` 'slabs' (moot without a flow).
- `mantle_flow.py`: `MantleFlow.placed_velocity()`: with `flow_power` 'measured' each degree's velocity × the factor its
  topography took (`self.scale`, kept by `update`): the flow of the same record with the loads of degree l scaled by
  that factor (topography and velocity are both linear in the load, kernel by kernel), so velocity and topography stay
  one flow at Earth's power; with 'flow' the flow's own `v_xyz`.
- `events.py`: `_cut_forces` adds the term when `rift_traction` is set and a flow exists.

#### 7. Order (told to the user in the reply)

But §1–2 say the target's gap at the degrees Earth constrains comes from where the continents are, and that every model
keeps them clustered. If the sweep does not disperse them (l 1 share 0.54, resultant 0.44, continent on one plate 104
against Earth's 0.25 / 0.31 / 42), the breakup and dispersal (§5 item 3: the split rule's lottery for plate-wide cuts,
the opening of rifted oceans after the split, the young rifts that close: 0.40 become passive against ~0.6–0.85) should
move ahead of the rest of item 3. That is a change of order and goes to the user.
