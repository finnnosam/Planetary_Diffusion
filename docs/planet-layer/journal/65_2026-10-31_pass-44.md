**Reading against the fixed rules.**
- R1 holds, strongly: Earth 0.37, planet 0.06, null 0.07 (p95 0.14). It holds in every class, which by the rule
  points at the force law or the start / split kinematics. Section 4 decides between them: on Earth's own plates the
  laws are at the null too, so the missing coherence is the law's (not the planet's start or splits). Earth's
  mechanism for it is known: plates ride a large-scale mantle flow driven by the slabs, and basal traction drives
  where the mantle moves faster than the plate (Hager & O'Connell 1981; Lithgow-Bertelloni & Richards 1998; Becker &
  O'Connell 2001; Conrad & Lithgow-Bertelloni 2002). The torque law's slab suction, which fitted to zero in pass 43,
  is only a local form of this.
- But section 4 also shows that the coherence is not what sets T2 on Earth's arrangement: the laws give Earth's slow
  closing there without it. The planet's T2 excess lies in its arrangement: its ocean–ocean convergence sits between
  fast, slab-bearing plates, and new trench appears quickly (42% within 10–20 Myr). Whether coherent motion would
  change that arrangement over 250 Myr (neighbours over one downwelling converging slowly, fewer boundaries turning
  into trench) is plausible but unmeasured.
- R2 fails (initiation pairs 7%; lines from different start plates 76%). R3 fails (9% vs 5%).

**Decision (Claude, by the goal and the fixed rule; not a user decision): no setting changed, no sweep; T2 is set
aside as a direct lever and D starts.**
- D's design (journal 55) is extended. If the slab record drives an instantaneous flow (radial viscosity, spherical
  harmonics; ducc0 does the transforms), the same flow gives the dynamic topography and the surface flow, and plates
  can feel traction toward it. That is the missing coherence's mechanism, from the same slabs.
- The D design pass decides kinematic kernel vs flow. Added to D's tests (fixed now, from this pass's Earth numbers):
  on Earth's plates, with the law plus flow traction, C(d) of different plates > 0 at < 2,000 km (Earth +0.21 … +0.25,
  law alone −0.12 … −0.24) and ρ < 0.5 share in Bird's slow classes toward Earth's (CRB 0.74, OCB 0.69; law alone
  0.14–0.28), without losing T-a … T-d. On the planet, read C(d), ρ by class, T2 and the io trench's age.
- Earth's present-day slabs for that test need a slab model (Slab2 or a subduction history). That is a data download
  for the user, to be named in the D pass.
- Realism, apart from T2: Earth's plate velocities are coherent and the planet's are not. That is a gap in its own
  right, measured now on both bodies.
