**2. Why the supply stays small: the threshold holds loose sediment.** The continents erode 3.4 km³/yr (rey, whole
run) and redeposit 3.1 of it on continental crust (90%): 1.6 under the sea (drowned margins, shelves), 1.4 above it.
The 46 km law (`graph_landscape.erode_and_deposit`, Yuan et al. 2019 with an incision threshold θ, fitted to OCTOPUS
¹⁰Be rates on ETOPO at this grid in pass 15) applies θ on every node: where A^m S is below θ = 200 a node is
deposition-only (v = z + G q_s / A), and nothing deposited there can leave by rivers again (hillslope diffusion only).
At 46 km most of the land's area is below θ (plains, plateaus): every sediment-carrying river drops part of its load
on every sub-threshold node it crosses, and that load is frozen. On Earth the threshold is bedrock's: the stream power
a river needs to cut rock (what ¹⁰Be basin rates measure, on bedrock catchments); loose sediment is entrained and
carried by far weaker flows (alluvial rivers are transport-limited, their beds move at a small excess of the
critical Shields stress; Parker 1978, Church 2006). So the model's threshold is right for the bedrock it was fitted on
and wrong for the sediment it is also applied to. That is why the planet keeps Earth's volume of sediment on land
above the sea but on high land (journal 108 §4), and why the sea's supply is short wherever it is measured (export
2.0 against 4–8; the margins 45 against 144 Mkm³).

**3. Built: `sediment_entrainment`** ('bedrock' default, as built; 'cover'). With 'cover' the plate layer passes the
sediment layer (`world['sed']`, continental and oceanic, bulk metres) as a loose cover to the erosion kernel
(`graph_landscape.erode_and_deposit_cover`, `surface_step(cover=…)`): on a covered node the fluvial step is solved
with θ = 0 (Yuan's law as the regional model has it) and the surface may not fall below the cover's base by that
path; where the bedrock law (with θ) cuts deeper it wins. The cover follows the surface over the erosion substeps
(deposits add, erosion takes it first). No new number: the same K, m, G, Sc. `erode_and_deposit` calls the new kernel
with a zero cover (exactly the old arithmetic), so the default path is unchanged; the 11.53 km landscape stage keeps
its law (it never passes a cover).
