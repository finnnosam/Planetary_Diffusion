#### 3. The arm built: PARAMS `remap_rule` 'nearest' (default) / 'tent' / 'overlap'

- 'tent' (`_tent_weights`): the composition interpolates crust thickness, sediment, rock, thermal age and formation age
  from the nearest plate node and its eight neighbours of the same crust type, each weighted linearly along its own
  direction (1 − |q − p_c| / |p_c − p_0|; the nearest node 1 − |q − p_0| / its shortest axis spacing; arc distances),
  normalised: exact at the nodes, a partition of unity. Crust type and orogeny age stay the nearest node's (the age's NaN
  is a validity mask). The per-area increments go back by the same weights (each plate node the weighted mean of the
  increments that reach it; a live owned node no weight reaches takes the world node over it where the crust type
  agrees), and each plate's gains and losses **per crust type** are rescaled to the world volume of its nodes of that
  type (`_conserved`). Collision volume, orogeny marks, thermal-age changes, arcs: as 'read'. Needs `return_transfer`
  'read'.
- 'overlap' (`_overlap_weights`), built after the tent trace (§4): first-order conservative remapping. Each world cell is
  sampled at nine points (its node and the points a third of the way to its eight neighbours), each read from its
  nearest plate node of the crust type (1/9 each): the cell's overlap with the plate cells. The return weights are those
  overlaps in area, so a plate cell takes the area mean of the increments over it, and the overlap weights reaching a
  plate cell sum to about its own area (median |Σ/a − 1| < 0.15 in the test). Same return path otherwise.
- Tests (`tests/test_remap_tent.py`, in `run_sweep.PLANET_TESTS`; 13 of 13, with `test_return_transfer` 22 of 22): the
  default 'nearest', unknown rules raise, 'tent' with another `return_transfer` raises; the weights are a partition of
  unity of one crust type and exact at the nodes (both rules); a smooth field is read closer to its truth than the
  nearest copy (rms < 0.6×); a uniform 100 m increment leaves every live owned node of each crust type uniform (spread
  < 0.05 m) with that type's volume the world's to 1e-4; a smooth increment field stays smooth (corr > 0.999); sediment
  gains and losses each conserved per plate; both ledgers close over 15 full steps with erosion.
- Identity of the default path (`rep.py`, n = 48, seed 1007, 70 steps, HEAD against the new code in the cloud): **71 of
  71 states identical at `threads` 1 and 71 of 71 at `threads` 2.** No default changed: no `PREDATING` key.
