**1. The data** (Hoggard, Czarnota, Hazell et al. 2020, Nat. Geosci. 13, 504; OSF https://osf.io/twksd; fetched by the
user into `data\Hoggard2020\`). Global 0.5° LAB depth from SL2013sv (their preferred model) and three comparison
models. Probes in `outputs/planet-calibration/precheck-p80-hoggard/`: `hoggard_first_read.py` (1° cells, medians),
`hoggard_law.py`, `hoggard_table.py` (the n = 217 nodes of Earth's continent as C defines it, `earth_continent`:
CRUST1.0 continental types, total ≥ 20 km, ice sheets out; area-weighted means, since the isostatic term is linear in L).

**2. The cooling form does not describe Earth's means.** A conductive plate-cooling law L_inf − (L_inf − L_a)
exp(−t/τ) fitted on the nodes runs τ to the fit's bound (1,500 Myr) on SL2013sv and SLNAAFSA (1,228–1,263 on the
others), rms 46–59 km: the plate-cooling τ for L_inf ≈ 190 km is ~105–128 Myr. Earth's class means are a step, not a
curve: SL2013sv 110 / 112 / 120 / 115 km for the 33 / 100 / 270 / 770 Ma classes, 165 / 184 / 185 / 162 km for
1.52 / 1.75 / 2.65 / 3.2 Ga (`hoggard_law.txt`). Cratonic roots were not grown by cooling over the Phanerozoic.

**3. The law as built: Earth's class means, unthickened, made monotone.** The Cenozoic class (orogens, arcs, rifts,
extended crust) mean is 110 km with crust > 45 km and 94 without: thickened belts (Tibet, Andes) stand on ~197 km.
The planet thickens collided lithosphere itself ('thicken'), so the age law is measured on crust ≤ 45 km. Where an
older class is thinner than a younger one the classes are pooled (area-weighted pool-adjacent-violators: conductive
cooling never thins lithosphere as it ages; pooling keeps the area mean). SL2013sv, ≤ 45 km:

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Linear between the classes, flat below 33 Ma and beyond 3.6 Ga, plus 1e-6 km per Myr so it inverts (stretching,
thickening). Model spread (CAM2016, 3D2015-07Sv, SLNAAFSA, same procedure): young 84–97 km, cratons 171–185 km,
reference 126–135 km (`hoggard_table.txt`). The density excess is Lamb's k × ρ_m = 10.753 × 3.3 = 35.5 kg/m³ (Lamb
reduced his elevations with seismic L of the same kind). Reference: the law's area mean on Earth's whole continent,
130.68 km (C's was 130.67: a coincidence). Top: 177.74 km, the thickening bound (`lith_max_km`'s role: thickened
lithosphere past the steady thickness of the oldest lithosphere is removed; Artemieva's frame had 237.6). No fitted
number.
