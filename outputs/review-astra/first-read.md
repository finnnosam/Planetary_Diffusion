# Before reading the planning record, 22 November 2026

From terrain_probe.py and terrain.csv, the latest six present-day grids have plausible bulk land, shallow shelf and low-elevation fractions. The largest continental discrepancy I see is spatial. Only 44% of dry land has four dry neighbour rings, versus 69% on the Earth comparison; low, flat terrain within those interiors occupies 8% versus 21%. Requiring both conditions makes the difference larger. At the other end only 0.19% of land exceeds 4 km versus 1.90% on Earth. The median local four-ring relief of dry interiors is close (655 versus 639 m), hiding the lowland failure. Land could be shredded by inland depressions or broken into small islands; this statistic cannot distinguish them. Oceans cover most of the map and their four-ring fully submerged relief is only 754 versus 1,410 m. Their young-floor area per 10 Myr is 4.98 versus 2.99 km²/yr. Missing seafloor structure and excessive production deserve at least as much attention as summit height.

The first long run changes the diagnosis. Between elapsed 250 and 500 Myr the number of plates rises from 40 to 64, continental fraction falls from 39.9% to 31.6%, continental crystalline volume falls from 7.08 to 5.90 billion km³, land mean rises from 889 to 1,342 m, and the thick tail grows markedly. I would investigate the material and area accounts, the imposed fragmentation rate and the connection between these and the geometry before altering highland strength. A stationary process or unique cause has not been demonstrated. Need check elapsed-time identity, run provenance and gate design.

The downstream comparison I found is from October. Its lowlands are scarce already in the conditioning set. It cannot establish the current meso result. Earth reference has under-ice negative values at Greenland and Antarctica in the raw raster despite the 'surface' filename; nonpolar benchmarks are preferable here. The 1-degree crust model is coarser than the altitude raster. Matching a global thickness histogram will not validate its association with regional relief.
